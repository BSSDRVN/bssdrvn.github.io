Adam Nordquist is an open-format DJ based in Minnesota for weddings, proms, formals, parties, school events, and whatever else needs a good soundtrack. He's been DJing for nearly a decade, and has experience with a wide range of music genres (especially electronic and 2010s pop/dance hits) and event types.

His alter alias, BSSDRVN, has been with him for his entire career. The BSSDRVN project focuses on electronic music, pushing the limits of what a copy of FL Studio and a few Serum 2 instances can do. BSSDRVN has released music on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Bandcamp, SoundCloud, and all other major streaming platforms and is available for licensing and collaborations.

PERFORMER

Trombonist

Adam Nordquist is also a trombonist performing in ensembles, concerts, and live events. He is a former section leader of the award-winning Gustavus Jazz Ensemble and Gustavus Wind Orchestra and a current player in the Bob Nordquist Band.

He is an impressive jazz improviser, reliable solo performer, and excellent session trombonist. He builds groups up and brings them to their limits. Need a smart, quick-thinking trombone player for your recording project? Call Adam.

Adam has contributed to a wide variety of recordings, including:

  • Drawing Inspiration, Toward the Horizons, and The River of Time, Gustavus Wind Orchestra
  • hope is not a language the dark voices understand, Moriah Margaret Jensen (link)
