About Adam Nordquist

Adam Nordquist is a Minnesota-based musician, DJ, and technology enthusiast with interests spanning music, creative production, software development, and data analytics.

As a musician, Adam performs on trombone and works across a variety of musical styles, from live performance and recording to electronic music production. He also works as an open-format DJ, providing music for weddings, private events, and other occasions.
