  • nordWAR

    An open-source baseball analytics framework designed to estimate Wins Above Replacement (WAR) in amateur and townball leagues. The project adapts established sabermetric concepts, including weighted On-Base Average (wOBA), weighted Runs Above Average (wRAA), positional adjustments, and league-specific constants, to leagues with limited statistical and defensive data.
